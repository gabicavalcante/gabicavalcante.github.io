Hi! My name is **Gabi Cavalcante**. I’m a full-stack engineer and tech lead based in Campinas, Brazil, with 9+ years of Python behind me — mostly Django, plus the data platform and infrastructure that sits around it.

<!--more-->

I work at [Vinta Software](https://www.vinta.com.br/), where I lead the Data Platform and Platform/Infrastructure team. Day to day that means API orchestration, data ingestion, and the reliability and security of the platform itself. Before this team I led a full-stack App Squad building with [Django](https://www.djangoproject.com/) and React, with my own focus on the backend architecture.

Some of the work I’ve enjoyed most there: owning the API architecture for integrations with external systems — including direct trademark office integrations — to extract and normalize global trademark data at scale; getting a query on a 25M-row PostgreSQL table from timing out down to ~54 seconds with histogram-based rewrites and keyset pagination; and putting together the technical evidence and architecture documentation for our SOC 2 Type II certification. More recently I built an AI-assisted code review skill that catches Django migrations likely to lock a table or cause downtime, before they get merged.

The other half of leading a team is not code at all: 1:1s, career conversations, mentoring, and running incident management and post-incident reviews when production has a bad day.

Before Vinta I was at [Stone](https://www.stone.com.br/), building developer-experience and compliance tooling on the Governance team, and at Cloudia, working on the backend of an intelligent virtual assistant for medical clinics — tests, stability, and moving large amounts of data between services. Along the way I’ve also written Python for payment integrations, facial recognition, multi-factor authentication over NFC, and a smart city research project.

But, stop talking about work. I’m graduated in Information Technology at the Federal University of Rio Grande do Norte (UFRN) with a Sandwich Degree (2015-2016) fully funded in France (Polytech Nice Sophia) by the exchange program BRAFITEC. I am also one of the founders of PyLadies Brasil. And I love coffee =)
